## Files and processing

The public HQ MP3 previews, rather than original lossless masters, are retained in `assets/audio/sources/`. The [source manifest](assets/audio/sources/manifest.json) records source/download URLs, author, license, and SHA-256 hashes. Source-page HTML retains the license evidence; embedded webpage scripts and map tokens have been removed.

Recordings are resampled to 44.1 kHz stereo PCM WAV, filtered for low-frequency rumble, level matched, and mastered with an equal-power wrap crossfade. Some recordings use gentle peak control to soften close-up impacts. Exact durations, levels, sample hashes, and seam differences are in [quality-report.json](assets/audio/quality-report.json).

`fetch_recordings.py` downloads the inputs; `generate_audio.py` regenerates the original loops and synthesis, then calls `master_additions.py` for the later recordings.

## Original synthesis

Brown Noise and Deep Space are generated by `scripts/generate_audio.py` and do not use third-party recordings.

## Original artwork

The illustrated sound covers are original project artwork generated from the SVG scenes in `scripts/generate_artwork.mjs`. No external photos, stock artwork, or third-party music is included in the current app.
